BLOCK RUSH 3D · Google Play Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

App Name: BLOCK RUSH 3D · Android Package ID: com.blockrush3d.game · Effective Date: November 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how BLOCK RUSH 3D (Android package com.blockrush3d.game) handles player data across its web/Progressive Web App (PWA) version and its native Android app built with Capacitor.

BLOCK RUSH 3D does not require user registration or accounts, and the developer does not operate external game servers, custom analytics collectors, or cloud profile databases. However, when running as a native Android app, embedded third-party Google advertising and consent SDKs communicate with Google services as described below.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Game progress and preferences are stored strictly locally on your device using browser or WebView localStorage (under key block_rush_3d_save_v1) and offline PWA asset caches. This local save data includes:

  • Player Profile: Your chosen display name (default: Runner), avatar icon/color selection, and earned rank title.
  • Gameplay & Progression: Virtual coins, best endless scores and distances, 50-stage completion stars, unlocked/equipped runners, skins, block themes, track worlds, daily login streak dates, missions, achievements, and aggregate play statistics.
  • App Preferences: Music/SFX enabled states and volumes, vibration/haptics setting, graphics quality tier, control hints toggle, and selected UI language.
  • Consent Metadata: Locally cached Google UMP consent status (consentStatus) and last update timestamp (lastUpdatedAt).

This local save data is never transmitted to developer-owned servers.

3. Offline Demo Leaderboards

The Global, Weekly, and Friends leaderboard views combine your local save scores with built-in offline demo benchmarks. No player scores, names, or device identifiers are uploaded to any leaderboard server.

4. Advertising, Google Mobile Ads SDK & UMP Consent

Web & PWA Mode: In browser and PWA mode, the Google Mobile Ads SDK is never initialized and no AdMob network requests are made.

Native Android App: The Android app integrates @capacitor-community/admob, which bundles Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK (play-services-ads) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK:

  • Consent Gating: Before initializing the Mobile Ads SDK or requesting any banner or rewarded ad, the app queries Google UMP (requestConsentInfo) and displays a consent form when required. Ads are requested only if UMP confirms canRequestAds === true. You can review or manage privacy consent from Settings.
  • Current Non-Personalized Test-Ads Mode: The current build requests Google sample test ad units with non-personalized ads enforced (npa: true) and personalized ad targeting disabled.
  • Data Processed by Google SDKs: Even when non-personalized ads are requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK and UMP SDK connect directly to Google servers and may automatically collect or process technical and advertising signals such as IP address (for general location/routing and compliance), Android Advertising ID (AD_ID) or app-set/device identifiers, device and OS metadata, ad impressions/clicks/viewability events, crash/performance diagnostics, invalid-traffic fraud prevention signals, and UMP consent records.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policies. For details, review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services.

5. Web Fonts & Offline Caching

To render display typography, the app loads stylesheets and font files from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com) and caches them locally via the PWA service worker. Standard web request metadata (such as IP address and user-agent) may be received by Google Fonts servers when fonts are fetched over the network.

6. Android App Permissions

The Android application and its integrated Capacitor/Google SDKs declare or merge only the permissions required for gameplay feedback, offline/ad networking, and ad compliance:

Permission Source Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET App Manifest & AdMob Plugin Required for Google UMP consent requests, Google Mobile Ads SDK requests on Android, and font loading.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE AdMob Plugin Checks network availability before ad/consent requests.
android.permission.VIBRATE Capacitor Haptics Plugin Optional tactile feedback during gameplay (can be disabled in Settings).
com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID Merged by Google Mobile Ads SDK Used by Google Mobile Ads SDK / Google Play services in accordance with UMP consent and Android Advertising ID controls.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call logs, or external storage permissions.

7. Children's Privacy

BLOCK RUSH 3D is a general-audience arcade game. The developer does not knowingly collect personal contact information, accounts, or user-submitted personal data from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region). If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us using the developer contact details below so we can assist promptly.

8. Your Privacy Controls & Data Deletion

  • Erase Local Save Data: Open Settings → Reset Game Progress inside the app at any time to permanently delete all locally stored profile names, coins, stars, unlocks, and preferences, or clear app/browser site data in your device settings.
  • Manage UMP Consent: Use the UMP consent / privacy options control in Settings on native Android to review or update your consent choices.
  • Reset or Delete Advertising ID: On Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google Settings → Ads) to reset or delete your device's Advertising ID.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time (for example, when transitioning from test ad units to production Google AdMob configuration or updating app features). Any updates will be posted on this page and reflected inside the app's Settings privacy view with an updated effective date.
